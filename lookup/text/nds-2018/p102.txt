12.5.1.3 Where D ≥ 1/4", edge distance and spac-
ing between rows of fasteners shall be in accordance
with Table 12.5.1C and Table 12.5.1D and applicable
requirements of 12.1. The perpendicular to grain dis-
tance between the outermost fasteners shall not exceed
5" (see Figure 12H) unless special detailing is provid-
ed to accommodate cross-grain shrinkage of the wood
member. For structural glued laminated timber mem-
bers, the perpendicular to grain distance between the
outermost fasteners shall not exceed the limits in Table
12.5.1F, unless special detailing is provided to ac-
commodate cross-grain shrinkage of the member.

12.5.1.4 Where fasteners are installed in the nar-
row edge of cross-laminated timber panels and D ≥
1/4", end distances, edge distances, and fastener spac-
ing in a row shall not be less than the minimum values
in Table 12.5.1G.
