15.3 Built-Up Columns

15.3.1 General

The following provisions apply to nailed or bolted
built-up columns with 2 to 5 laminations in which:

(a) each lamination has a rectangular cross section

and is at least 1-1/2" thick, t ≥ 1-1/2".
(b) all laminations have the same depth (face

width), d.
(c) faces of adjacent laminations are in contact.
(d) all laminations are full column length.
(e) the connection requirements in 15.3.3 or 15.3.4

are met.
Nailed or bolted built-up columns not meeting the
preceding limitations shall have individual laminations
designed in accordance with 3.6.3 and 3.7. Where indi-
vidual laminations are of different species, grades, or
thicknesses, the lesser adjusted compression parallel to
grain design value, Fc', and modulus of elasticity for

beam and column stability, Emin', for the weakest lami-
nation shall apply.
