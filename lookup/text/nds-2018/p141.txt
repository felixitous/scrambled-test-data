(b.2)
Loaded Edge Distance. The minimum
loaded edge distance parallel to the axis of
cut for CΔ = 1.0 shall be determined in ac-
cordance with Equation 13.3-3.

For split rings, the minimum loaded
edge distance parallel to the axis of cut for
CΔ = 0.70 shall be determined in accord-
ance with Equation 13.3-3. For shear
plates, the minimum loaded edge distance
parallel to the axis of cut for CΔ = 0.83
shall be determined in accordance with
Equation 13.3-3.

Where the actual loaded edge distance
parallel to the axis of cut is greater than the
minimum loaded edge distance parallel to
the axis of cut for CΔ = 0.70 for split rings
or for CΔ = 0.83 for shear plates, but less
than the minimum loaded edge distance
parallel to the axis of cut for CΔ = 1.0, the
geometry factor, CΔ, shall be determined by
linear interpolation.

(b.3)
Unloaded Edge Distance. The mini-
mum unloaded edge distance parallel to the
axis of cut for CΔ = 1.0, shall be determined
in accordance with Equation 13.3-4.

The minimum unloaded edge distance
parallel to the axis of cut for CΔ = 0.63
shall be determined in accordance with
Equation 13.3-4.

Where the actual unloaded edge dis-
tance parallel to the axis of cut is greater
than the minimum unloaded edge distance
for CΔ = 0.63, but less than the minimum
unloaded edge distance for CΔ = 1.0, the
geometry factor, CΔ, shall be determined by
linear interpolation.
