Reference withdrawal design values, W, for con-
nections using drift bolt and drift pin connections shall
be determined in accordance with 11.1.1.3.

tns, given in Table 12.2F. Reference pull-through design
values, WH, shall be multiplied by all applicable ad-
justment factors (see Table 11.3.1) to obtain adjusted
pull-through design values, W'H.
