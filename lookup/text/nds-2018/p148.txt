136 TIMBER RIVETS

14.3 Placement of Timber Rivets

14.3.1 Spacing Between Rivets

Minimum spacing of rivets shall be 1/2" perpen-
dicular to grain, sq, and 1" parallel to grain, sp. The
maximum distance perpendicular to grain between
outermost rows of rivets shall be 12".

14.3.2 End and Edge Distance

Minimum values for end distance (ap, aq) and edge
distance (ep, eq) as shown and noted in Figure 14A, are
listed in Table 14.3.2.
