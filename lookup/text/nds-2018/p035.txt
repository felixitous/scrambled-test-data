3.10 Design for Bearing

3.10.1 Bearing Parallel to Grain

3.10.1.1 The actual compressive bearing stress par-
allel to grain shall be based on the net bearing area and
shall not exceed the reference compression design value
parallel to grain multiplied by all applicable adjustment
factors except the column stability factor, CP.

3.10.1.2 Fc

provided there is adequate lateral support and the end
cuts are accurately squared and parallel.

inserted with a snug fit between abutting ends.

3.10.2 Bearing Perpendicular to Grain

The actual compression stress perpendicular to
grain shall be based on the net bearing area and shall
not exceed the adjusted compression design value per-
pendicular to grain, fc⊥ ≤ Fc⊥'. When calculating bearing
area at the ends of bending members, no allowance
shall be made for the fact that as the member bends,
pressure upon the inner edge of the bearing is greater
than at the member end.
