Abottom plane net = (Abottom plane) – (Asplit ring groove) –

(Abolt hole)

= (6.75")(2.92") – (π/4)[(2.92")2–(2.92" –

0.18" – 0.18")2] – (π/4)(0.5625")2

= 17.91 in.2

Acriticial = Aedge plane + Abottom plane net

= 5.063 + 17.91 in.2 = 22.973 in.2
