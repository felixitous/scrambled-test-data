12.4 Combined Lateral and Withdrawal Loads

12.4.1 Lag Screws and Wood Screws

Where a lag screw or wood screw is subjected to
combined lateral and withdrawal loading, as when the
fastener is inserted perpendicular to the fiber and the
load acts at an angle, α, to the wood surface (see Figure
12F), the adjusted design value, Zα', shall be deter-
mined as follows (see Appendix J):

                  (W′p)Z′
    Zα′=
          (W′p)cos2  α+  Z′ sin2 α

           direction of applied load, degrees
       p = length of thread penetration into the main
           member, in.

12.5 Adjustment of Reference Design Values

12.5.1 Geometry Factor, CΔ

12.5.1.1 For dowel-type fasteners where D < 1/4",
CΔ = 1.0.

12.5.1.2 Where D ≥ 1/4" and the end distance or
spacing provided for dowel-type fasteners is less than
the minimum required for CΔ = 1.0 for any condition in
(a), (b), or (c), reference lateral design values, Z, shall
be multiplied by the smallest applicable geometry fac-
tor, CΔ, determined in (a), (b), or (c). The smallest ge-
ometry factor for any fastener in a group shall apply to
all fasteners in the group. For multiple shear connec-
tions or for asymmetric three member connections, the
smallest geometry factor, CΔ, for any shear plane shall
apply to all fasteners in the connection.
